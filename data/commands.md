# The Eldritch Ledger: Chat Commands

*Madness is earned by chatting, checking in, banishing Beings and supporting the stream. Pacts and Eldritch Pacts are rarer, and worth more. Most commands work on Twitch, YouTube and Kick.*

## 🕯 The Delve
A run into the dark that lasts: push deeper five depths at a time, carry your haul, and get out before the dark gets you. Works in stream chat and in Discord **#descent**, and it's the same run in both.
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!delve shallow 500` | Start a run on the Shallow ladder (also `deep` and `abyss`; Deep opens at Shallow 50, the Abyss at Deep 50). The dark takes 5% at the entrance |
| `!push` | Go 5 depths deeper: three rooms of monsters, traps, treasure and stranger things. Costs 1 Lantern Oil |
| `!push boon` / `!push charm:aegis` | A boon for this push (1 Pact, or a Charm): Steady, Greed, Aegis, Pathfinder or Second Wind. It may also turn your weapon legendary or Golden for the rest of the run. `!delve boons` explains them |
| `!surface` | Climb out with your stake and everything you found |
| `!delve` | Your run: depth, wounds, haul, oil. Without a run: your oil and waystones |
| `!delve use salve` / `!delve use oil` | Heal a wound, or refill your lantern |
| `!delve deep 2000 from 40` | Start at a waystone you've reached (one every 10 depths, yours forever) |
| `!delve shallow 500 lure:grave` | Bring a lure: mostly that enemy type, more of its materials, and its lord waits at the bottom |
| `!deepest` | The deepest delvers, and who has broken the Bottom |
| `!delve rank` | Your **Delver Rank**: XP, the next rank, your fight edge and perks |
| `!delve perk` / `!delve perk lamplighter` | List the perks, or pick one (a pick every 5 ranks, each perk once). `!delve respec` gives them all back for 1 Eldritch Pact |
| `!delve feed 500` | Feed your XP to Tsathoggua (only progress toward your next rank; your rank never drops). 100 XP fills him by 1 Madness |
| `!delve gear` / `!delve equip harpoon` / `!delve repair` | Your **gear**: a weapon, garb and trinket you forge in Discord (`/forge craft`, Delve Gear). A crafted weapon is +2% in every fight (+3% against its kind), garb and trinkets dodge traps, spare wounds, turn dead ends aside and more. Gear wears out over runs; a broken piece is mended with half its commons, never lost. Each cult has its own piece |
| `!delve around` | At depth 50 every ladder has a **Gatekeeper**. Beat it for a Gatekeeper's Sigil (for Gatekeeper gear) and a lot of XP, or pay 1 oil to sneak past it with nothing |
| `!delve branch` | Take a **Crack in the World** when one opens (Deep 30–60, Deep 40–80, Abyss 1–30): 30 depths of the **Underworld**, the **Sunken Halls of Y'ha-nthlei** or the **Plateau of Leng**, with their own enemies, materials, curios and a boss at the end (haul ×1.5). Bring the branch's tool: a Bone Lantern (or ghasts ambush you), Gill-Draughts (or you drown a little every push), a Woven Rope of Leng. `!delve branches` shows the ones you know; `!delve underworld 500` starts straight in one you've found. Leng only opens through a Dreamland quest that reaches it |
| `!dreamquest atal ulthar seek 500` | Send a dreamer into the **Dreamlands** (yourself, Atal, Kuranes, Pickman or Randolph Carter) to Gather, Seek or Deliver. It takes hours of real time; halfway, a choice waits in the Discord forge channel. `!dreamquest` shows where they are, `!dreamquest help` and `!dreamquest regions` the rest |
| `!cast hand @user` | Cast a spell on someone in the Delve. `!spells` lists them: the Hand takes a wound (never the last), Dim the Lantern steals oil, the Yellow Sign darkens their next push, Tsathoggua's Slumber heals one of their wounds, the Veil spares their next lost fight. Costs Pacts and Void Dust. If they're not in the Delve, nothing is spent |
| `!cast @user` | A wild cast: a random spell for 1 Pact. It might help them. It might hit you |

Three wounds and you fall: the dark keeps your stake and your haul, and a fifth of it stays where you fell for the next delver to find. A **Ward** you carry in drags you out with your whole stake (a Lesser Ward with half). Hit a **dead end** without a **Rope** and the way down is shut: you have to surface, with your stake and your haul, and the run is over. Carry Ropes (or roll Pathfinder) if you mean to go deep. At depth 100 waits **the Bottom**: beat its lord to double your haul. Oil comes back 1 every 30 minutes, up to 8. Forge Torches, Ropes, Salves, Smoke, Lockpicks and Waystone Shards in Discord (category Delve), or buy them from the Merchant, to go deeper.

**Delver Rank.** Every run earns XP: 10 / 20 / 30 per push on the Shallow / Deep / Abyss ladder (if you fall, the dark keeps half of it), and when you surface alive half the depth you reached, ×1 / ×2 / ×3, plus 300 / 600 / 900 for the Bottom. Subs and members earn 10–30% more, and a happy hour multiplies it. Each rank gives a little more luck in every fight (up to +3%), and every 5 ranks you pick a perk: more oil, a Rope that sometimes isn't used up, a fourth wound, a Last Breath that drags you out of one death per run, and more. Beware the toad: while **Tsathoggua** goes hungry (nobody has fed him this stream), he licks 10% of the XP off every surfacing. The Sleepers of N'kai pay nothing, and once he's full the tax stops. Your **cult** matters down there too: the higher your rank, the more often a material drop comes from your cult's own types (up to 25%), and members of the Esoteric Order earn up to 15% more XP.

## 🌺 Pickman, Ghoul for Hire *(new)*
Richard Upton Pickman, in a tattered Hawaiian shirt, fetches forge materials for you. He comes back by himself hours later (even when the stream is off), eats a little of the haul, and now and then paints you instead. One job at a time. Works in chat and in Discord **#gibbering-forge** (`/pickman`).
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!pickman` | Where he is, your Favour and his snack tax |
| `!pickman rummage` | 500 Madness: Grave materials, sometimes more (2 h) |
| `!pickman fetch <type>` | 1 Pact: commons of a type you name, e.g. `fetch moon` (3 h) |
| `!pickman order <rare>` | 1 Eldritch Pact, Patron and up: two of a rare you name (5 h) |

| `!pickman swap <from> <to>` | 5 commons of one type for 2 of another (1 h) |
| `!pickman feed <material> [n]` | Feed him Grave materials for Favour (he gets full each week) |
| `!pickman trade` | Give him spare curios for Favour |
| `!pickman curios` / `!pickman gallery` | Your curio sets / the paintings he made of you |
| `!pickman shirt` | At Friend of the Ghouls, he lends you his spare shirt (once). It does nothing in a fight. It does make your dreams bring home a little more |

**Favour** (Stranger, Regular, Patron, Friend of the Ghouls, Pickman's Muse) makes him eat less, work faster and take bigger orders. It grows slowly: finished jobs, feeding him, spare curios and subs. Collect all six of **Pickman's Lost Sketches** for a title.

**Curios** turn up in Delve chests and packs, from Gatekeepers, in branches, on Pickman's jobs and in dreams. Eight sets: Pickman's Lost Sketches, the Pnakotic Manuscripts, the Whateley Heirlooms, the Marsh Regalia, the Tapestries of Leng, the Relics of the Miskatonic, the Cats of Ulthar trinkets, and the **Relics of the Esoteric Order** (only Order members find those). A full set gives a title and one small lasting perk (never fight odds); spare pieces go to Pickman for Favour.

## 👁 Nemeses
Some things in the dark don't forget. Lose a descent and the horror that beat you may survive and remember you: it gets a name, an epithet and a grudge, and it will hunt you on later descents. Every time it wins it grows stronger and learns something new. Every trait is shown, so you know what you're facing.
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!nemesis` | Your nemeses, their traits, and your Vendettas and Fears against them |
| `!nemesis <name>` | One nemesis in detail |
| `!nemesis board` | The captains of the dark and the Warchief |
| `!nemesis rivals` | Your duel rivalries: who torments you, and whom you torment |
| `!nemesis bounties` | The bounties on Tormentors' heads |
| `!nemesis titles` | Your *Slayer of …* titles |
| `!nemesis trophies` | The trophies you took from slain nemeses *(new)* |
| `!hunt <nemesis> 500` | Burn a **Named Lure** (forged in Discord) and that one nemesis answers your descent, guaranteed. Add `deep`/`abyss`, `boon` or `charm` like a normal descent *(new)* |

After a loss you carry a **Vendetta** (better odds, more winnings, or a second try) or a **Fear** (worse odds) against it. A stronger nemesis is harder to beat but pays more. Slay it for a big bonus, its trophy material and the title *Slayer of …*, but some crawl back, scarred. Forge a **Bane** in Discord and it is used by itself the next time a nemesis finds you (+6%). *(New:)* a slain nemesis leaves a named **trophy**; a nemesis that beats you leaves a **Dread** on you for 15 minutes (a little worse odds in duels, the Delve and against every nemesis); and your nemeses may wait for you in the **Delve**.

The nemesis with the most victims becomes the **Warchief**. Once a month the streamer can call it out with `!summon warchief`: swear in with `!oath` and bring it down together for a bigger payout, Madness and a Pact for everyone.

**Rivalries:** win three more duels (250 Madness or more) than you lose against someone and you become their **Tormentor**: a mark that pays extra on every duel you win, and a bounty on your head for whoever beats you. Your victim gets a Vendetta against you. Win your way back and you're free.

## ⭕ Summoning Circles
Want Dave to play a certain game? Feed its **summoning circle**. When a circle is full it is **bound**: Dave owes it time in that game before its deadline, and the clock counts by itself while he plays it. Overfill a circle and he owes it more time. If he misses the deadline, the circle breaks and everyone gets their offering back with 10% extra Madness.
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!circles` | Every circle: its game, how full it is, deadlines and proposals |
| `!circle 3` | One circle in detail: progress, the time it will demand, and its summoners |
| `!circle 3 5000` | Offer Madness to circle 3 (also `5k`, `10%` or `all`) |
| `!circle 3 pact` / `!circle 3 2 pacts` / `!circle 3 eldritch` | Offer Pacts or an Eldritch Pact (worth far more than Madness) |
| `!circle mine` | What you've offered |
| `!circle history` | The circles kept and broken so far |
| `!circle propose <game>` | Propose a new circle for 1 Pact (you get it back if it's turned down) |

Real money counts most: put `circle3` in the message of a cheer, Super Chat, KICKs, Ko-fi or Fourthwall tip of at least 1 EUR (70 bits / 70 KICKs) and it feeds circle 3. The first time Dave plays a bound circle's game in a stream, a **Summoning Hour** happy hour begins. When a circle is fulfilled, its biggest summoner gets a Pact and picks the first Pact Wheel challenge.

## 🐫 The Merchant from Leng
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!wares` | See what the Merchant is selling. He stays a few minutes per visit |
| `!buy 2` | Buy item #2 while stock lasts |

Not every stream draws him. When his **sigil** burns on screen, he is on his way: his first visit falls somewhere after the first 20 minutes, and he comes back about once every hour and a half. Besides relics he sells forge goods (materials, lures, Gate Keys, Delve tools, Charms and more), which go straight to your `!satchel`.

Now and then he comes as the **Gilded Merchant**: more wares, rarer goods you can't get from him otherwise, lower prices and a longer stay. A treasure goblin rides with him and slips Stolen Coins to some of his buyers.
