# The Eldritch Ledger: Chat Commands

*Madness is earned by chatting, checking in, banishing Beings and supporting the stream. Pacts and Eldritch Pacts are rarer, and worth more. Most commands work on Twitch, YouTube and Kick.*


## 📜 Your Ledger

| Command | What it does | 
| ----- | ----- | 
| `!madness` | Your Madness, Pacts, Eldritch Pacts and rank | 
| `!pacts` | Your Pacts and Eldritch Pacts | 
| `!rank` | Your place on the Madness leaderboard | 
| `!relics` | Every relic you carry: Wards, Elder Signs, Pages, Keys and more |

| `!codex` | Your achievements |

| `!forge <code>` | Link your Discord account (get the code with `/forge link` in Discord) | 

Your first message each stream signs the ledger automatically. You get a check-in reward, a chance at an **omen** (a boon or a curse), and milestone rewards at check-in #10, #25, #50, #100 and #200.

**✨ Legendaries:** now and then fate hands someone a legendary weapon: Andúril, Mjölnir, the Shining Trapezohedron, Stormbringer, the BFG 9000 and 40 more. If no legend answers, the boon may still **gild** your weapon Golden (+4%), or now and then Golden Eldritch (+7%). In the deep and the abyss, legendary horrors lurk (the Xenomorph, Pyramid Head, Nemesis, Malenia…). They're harder to beat, but they pay more.

## 🕯 The Delve

A run into the dark that lasts: push deeper five depths at a time, carry your haul, and get out before the dark gets you. Works in stream chat and in Discord **#descent**, and it's the same run in both.

| Command | What it does | 
| ----- | ----- | 
| `!delve shallow 500` | Start a run on the Shallow ladder (also `deep` and `abyss`; Deep opens at Shallow 50, the Abyss at Deep 50). The dark takes 5% at the entrance | 
| `!push` | Go 5 depths deeper: three rooms of monsters, traps, treasure and stranger things. Costs 1 Lantern Oil | 
| `!push boon` / `!push charm:aegis` | A boon for this push (1 Pact, or a Charm): Steady, Greed, Aegis, Pathfinder or Second Wind. It may also turn your weapon legendary or Golden for the rest of the run. `!delve boons` explains them | 
| `!surface` | Climb out with your stake and everything you found | 
| `!delve` | Your run: depth, wounds, haul, oil. Without a run: your oil and waystones | 
| `!delve use salve` / `!delve use oil` | Heal a wound, or refill your lantern | 
| `!delve deep 2000 from 40` | Start at a waystone you've reached (one every 10 depths, yours forever) | 
| `!delve shallow 500 lure:grave` | Bring a lure: mostly that enemy type, more of its materials, and its lord waits at the bottom | 
| `!deepest` | The deepest delvers, and who has broken the Bottom | 

Three wounds and you fall: the dark keeps your stake and your haul, and a fifth of it stays where you fell for the next delver to find. A **Ward** you carry in drags you out with your whole stake (a Lesser Ward with half). Hit a **dead end** without a **Rope** and the way down is shut: you have to surface, with your stake and your haul, and the run is over. Carry Ropes (or roll Pathfinder) if you mean to go deep. At depth 100 waits **the Bottom**: beat its lord to double your haul. Oil comes back 1 every 30 minutes, up to 8. Forge Torches, Ropes, Salves, Smoke, Lockpicks and Waystone Shards in Discord (category Delve), or buy them from the Merchant, to go deeper.

## 👁 Nemeses

Some things in the dark don't forget. Lose a descent and the horror that beat you may survive and remember you: it gets a name, an epithet and a grudge, and it will hunt you on later descents. Every time it wins it grows stronger and learns something new. Every trait is shown, so you know what you're facing.

| Command | What it does | 
| ----- | ----- | 
| `!nemesis` | Your nemeses, their traits, and your Vendettas and Fears against them | 
| `!nemesis <name>` | One nemesis in detail | 
| `!nemesis board` | The captains of the dark and the Warchief | 
| `!nemesis rivals` | Your duel rivalries: who torments you, and whom you torment | 
| `!nemesis bounties` | The bounties on Tormentors' heads | 
| `!nemesis titles` | Your *Slayer of …* titles | 
| `!nemesis trophies` | The trophies you took from slain nemeses *(new)* | 
| `!hunt <nemesis> 500` | Burn a **Named Lure** (forged in Discord) and that one nemesis answers your descent, guaranteed. Add `deep`/`abyss`, `boon` or `charm` like a normal descent *(new)* | 

After a loss you carry a **Vendetta** (better odds, more winnings, or a second try) or a **Fear** (worse odds) against it. A stronger nemesis is harder to beat but pays more. Slay it for a big bonus, its trophy material and the title *Slayer of …*, but some crawl back, scarred. Forge a **Bane** in Discord and it is used by itself the next time a nemesis finds you (+6%). *(New:)* a slain nemesis leaves a named **trophy**; a nemesis that beats you leaves a **Dread** on you for 15 minutes (a little worse odds in duels, the Delve and against every nemesis); and your nemeses may wait for you in the **Delve**.

The nemesis with the most victims becomes the **Warchief**. Once a month the streamer can call it out with `!summon warchief`: swear in with `!oath` and bring it down together for a bigger payout, Madness and a Pact for everyone.

**Rivalries:** win three more duels (250 Madness or more) than you lose against someone and you become their **Tormentor**: a mark that pays extra on every duel you win, and a bounty on your head for whoever beats you. Your victim gets a Vendetta against you. Win your way back and you're free.

## ⭕ Summoning Circles

Want Dave to play a certain game? Feed its **summoning circle**. When a circle is full it is **bound**: Dave owes it time in that game before its deadline, and the clock counts by itself while he plays it. Overfill a circle and he owes it more time. If he misses the deadline, the circle breaks and everyone gets their offering back with 10% extra Madness.

| Command | What it does | 
| ----- | ----- | 
| `!circles` | Every circle: its game, how full it is, deadlines and proposals | 
| `!circle 3` | One circle in detail: progress, the time it will demand, and its summoners | 
| `!circle 3 5000` | Offer Madness to circle 3 (also `5k`, `10%` or `all`) | 
| `!circle 3 pact` / `!circle 3 2 pacts` / `!circle 3 eldritch` | Offer Pacts or an Eldritch Pact (worth far more than Madness) | 
| `!circle mine` | What you've offered | 
| `!circle history` | The circles kept and broken so far | 
| `!circle propose <game>` | Propose a new circle for 1 Pact (you get it back if it's turned down) | 

Real money counts most: put `circle3` in the message of a cheer, Super Chat, KICKs, Ko-fi or Fourthwall tip and it feeds circle 3. The first time Dave plays a bound circle's game in a stream, a **Summoning Hour** happy hour begins. When a circle is fulfilled, its biggest summoner gets a Pact and picks the first Pact Wheel challenge.
