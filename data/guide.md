# The Ledger: what everything is

This file explains the stream's systems on eldritzh.com. Each `##` heading below matches a section on the commands page (emoji and punctuation don't matter) and shows there as **What is this?**. Headings that start with `Page:` are the intro of a whole page. Edit freely: the site follows on the next push. Anything between `<!-- MOD START -->` and `<!-- MOD END -->` is staff-only and never goes public.

## Page: commands
Everything you can type in chat, on Twitch, YouTube and Kick. Your currencies and progress are shared across all three once you `!link` your accounts. Open **What is this?** under any section for how that part of the stream works.

## Page: story
The Eldritch Ledger is the congregation's shared story. Every page is sealed until the whole chat reaches its goal together: check-ins, banished Beings, Madness fed to Tsathoggua, chants, even Dave's deaths. Some pages want several things at once, some must be earned in a single night, some only open on a Moon Night, during the Sunwait or after midnight, and a few wait for an earlier page or a vote of the congregation. Nothing here is bought; it is written by everyone playing along. Type `!story goals` in chat to see which pages are closest.

## Page: pacts
Pacts are challenges you put on Dave. `!pact` spends a Pact to spin the wheel over the list of the game he is playing; whatever it lands on is sealed until he pulls it off. `!epact` lets you write your own challenge with an Eldritch Pact. Every sealed Pact also feeds a Madness pool that is paid out to chat when the stream ends.

## Page: circles
Summoning circles let chat choose what Dave plays. Pledge Madness, Pacts or real offerings to a game's circle. When it reaches its target the circle is **bound**: Dave owes it that much stream time in that game before the deadline. If he misses it, every offering comes back with extra Madness on top.

## Page: codex
The Codex is the achievement book: hundreds of Revelations for chatting, banishing, duelling, delving, feeding and more, in ten tiers from Whisper to the Court of Azathoth. Higher tiers pay Madness, Pacts and Eldritch Pacts, and some give a title that chat greets you with. Linked accounts count together. `!forget` (or `!codex hide`) takes you off this page and every other page here.

## Page: partners
The stream's paid partners. Their links and codes support the channel, and their chat commands are listed with them.

## Page: sounds
Sounds you can play on stream by typing their name in chat, like `!steven`. Each sound has its own price, cooldown and audience: some are for everyone, some for subscribers and members, some for the special folks (others can still unlock those for a Pact). Cheering bits, a Super Chat or KICKs with `!name` in the message plays it too, and happy hour makes Madness prices cheaper. One sound plays at a time.

## Page: bestiary
Everything that lives in the dark beneath the stream. **Beings** break through onto the stream itself: type a Being's name in chat to banish it for Madness and Ichor. **The families** are what you meet when you `!descend`, raid or `!delve`: every enemy, the depth it lurks at, the lord at the bottom and the materials it drops for the Gibbering Forge. **Legendary horrors** turn up rarely in the deep and the abyss, or as raid bosses.

## Page: congregation
The state of the congregation right now: which cult rules this month, how full Tsathoggua is and who feeds him most, the nemeses hunting the dark, and the Clipomania champions. Updated from the stream as it happens.

## Page: hub
Everything the stream keeps, written down. Pick a page.

## Page: investigator
Everything you do on stream earns XP: turning up, playing the games, supporting. XP becomes levels, levels bring titles, talent points and a frame for your name. Look anyone up by chat name in the box below; `!forget` in chat takes you off every page here.

## Page: covenant
All the XP everyone earns wakes Eldritzh, level by level. Every Awakening level gives the congregation one point, and chat votes on what it buys: a talent that then counts for everyone, for good.

## Page: fishing
Cast a line with `!fish` or the *Go Fishing* reward. There are waters from Innsmouth Harbour to the Sea of Stars, each with its own fish and one legendary. Nothing is written in the journal below until somebody catches it.

## Page: rift
Rift Shards are effects you keep for later: get one now and fire it on stream when you like.

## Page: patchnotes
What's new on the stream, in Discord and on this site. Newest first.

## Your Ledger
Your account on the stream. You earn **Madness** by chatting, checking in, banishing Beings and playing the games; it is the everyday currency. **Pacts** are rarer and buy the big things (wheel spins, boons, unlocks). **Eldritch Pacts** are the rarest: five Pacts bind into one with `!ascend`. Your first message each stream checks you in automatically. `!link` joins your Twitch, Kick and YouTube accounts into one ledger, and `!forge` links Discord for crafting.

## Games of Madness
Ways to risk Madness for more. `!tempt` is a straight gamble. `!rite` challenges another viewer to a duel: both stake the same amount and the winner takes it. `!descend` sends you alone into the dark against a random enemy; deeper is deadlier and pays more. A **boon** (1 Pact) gives you an edge in a duel or descent, and now and then fate hands someone a legendary weapon. Raids (`!oath`) are the whole chat against one horror together.

## The Delve
The Delve is a longer run into the dark that lasts between messages: push five depths at a time, carry what you find, and climb out with `!surface` before three wounds take you. Lantern Oil limits how often you can push, waystones let you start deeper next time, and depth 100 holds the Bottom and its lord. It works the same in stream chat and in Discord #descent.

## Nemeses
Lose a descent and the thing that beat you may survive and remember you. It gets a name, an epithet and traits, and it hunts you on later descents, growing stronger every time it wins. Slay it for a big bonus, its trophy and the title *Slayer of …*. The nemesis with the most victims becomes the **Warchief**, which the whole chat can be called to raid. Duelists who keep beating the same person become their **Tormentor**.

## Wards, Ichor & Beings
**Beings** break through onto the stream now and then; a hint in chat warns that something stirs. Type the Being's name to banish it for Madness and Ichor. **Ichor** drops when Beings are banished. Boil it into Lesser Wards (`!craft`), pour it into a Being on screen for a bigger reward (`!pour`), or spend it to summon one (`!invoke`). **Wards** soften your losses in gambles and duels automatically, and can drag you out of a lost Delve.

## Rites & Relics
Relics are the rarer things you carry besides your currencies: Elder Signs that turn curses aside, Necronomicon Pages (13 bind into the book), Ritual Candles for opening circles that give the whole chat a happy hour, Silver Keys that skip queues and rests, and Tsathoggua's Favour. `!relics` shows what you have; `!rites` lists what you can do with them.

## Pacts Between Souls
Ways to tie yourself to another viewer. A **bind** siphons a share of what they earn this stream. A **Blood Pact** has you both earn a little of what the other earns until one of you breaks it. A **pledge** swears you to a Being so you share in every banish of it.

## Feed Tsathoggua
Tsathoggua, the sleeping toad-god, sits in the corner of the stream. Feed him Madness, Pacts or Sanity (channel points). Fill him and he sleeps for the rest of the stream, the top feeders are rewarded, and his dreams spill out as **Blessings of N'kai**: more Madness, double Ichor, cheaper prices and more for everyone. The more often he is filled, the hungrier he gets.

## The Pact Wheel
The wheel holds every challenge on the current game's list. `!pact` spins it for 1 Pact and seals what it lands on; `!epact` seals one you write yourself with an Eldritch Pact. Up to seven normal and three Eldritch Pacts can be open at once. `!pactadd` adds new challenges to the game's list (free while the list is short). Gold jackpot slices bless the whole chat.

## Banned Items
Ban something from Dave's next matches: a weapon, a tactic, a habit. It costs Madness (or a Pact) and lasts a number of matches. You can change or take back your ban for free until its first match has counted.

## Queue & Giveaways
The viewer queue is how you get to play with Dave: `!join` costs Madness (free for some groups), and you get it back if you `!leave`. Giveaways are entered with `!ticket`; subscribers and members get better odds, and prizes with codes are sent privately through Discord.

## Omens & Curses
Every check-in rolls an omen: usually a small boon, sometimes a minor curse. Curses can be lifted by paying Madness, a Pact, by someone else cleansing you, or by chanting together.

## Effects, Support & Patrons
`!fx` spends Pacts on effects that hit the stream and Dave's game live: eyes, ichor, tentacles and worse on screen, sounds, and the hands of the void that move Dave in-game. The disruptive ones cost an Eldritch Pact, and chat gets a few seconds to `!seal` them with an Elder Sign. Some effects need three of you to open a ritual circle together, some belong to one cult's faithful, and `!fx roll` lets the rift choose (it sometimes bites back). Every effect makes the rift more unstable; push it far enough and a Rift Storm brings everything through at once. Ko-fi and Fourthwall support is bound to your account with `!claim`, so it counts toward your rewards like bits and subs do.

## The Merchant from Leng
A travelling merchant who visits some streams. When his sigil burns on screen he is on his way; while he is here, `!wares` shows his stock and `!buy` takes it while it lasts. Rarely, the Gilded Merchant comes instead, with better wares and a treasure goblin.

## Death Rites
When Dave dies in-game, chat can mourn him within 20 seconds for a little Madness. `!fallen` keeps the tally.

## The Cults
Join one of eight cults with `!cult join`. Each has a small boon of its own. Everything you do earns glory for your cult, and the cult with the most glory at the end of the month is crowned and its faithful are rewarded.

## Prophecies & Bounties
**Prophecies** are bets on what happens next on stream; winners split the losers' stakes. **Bounties** are challenges you post for Dave with Madness behind them; if he pulls it off, every backer gets 150% back.

## Dreams, Haunts & the Old Words
Send your dreamer on an expedition into the Dreamlands and hear what they found half a day later. Haunt someone with a whisper that finds them the next time they speak. And when enough voices chant the old words together, something answers.

## Dave's Sanity
Dave has a Sanity meter that chat can drain with Madness, bits, subs and gifts. When it hits zero he suffers a Madness Episode on stream.

## The Stars Are Right
Now and then a Great Old One rises. Everything the chat does hurts it: chatting, banishing, feeding, bits, subs and `!strike`. Bring it down before the stars turn and every fighter is rewarded.

## The Gibbering Forge (Discord)
The forge is the stream's crafting workshop, in Discord. Materials drop from descents, raids and the Delve; craft them into Wards, Elder Signs, Candles, lures, Delve tools and more with `/forge`. What you craft arrives on stream within a minute.

## Yog-Sothoth's Hourglass
Timed challenges on Dave. `!challenge` buys minutes of a dare (no healing, pistols only…) with Pacts or Madness, and the Hourglass on stream counts it down. Anyone can `!extend` it. If he holds to the last grain, the Pact Wheel pool grows; if he breaks it, everyone who paid gets their price back with Madness on top. The sand stops while he's on a break screen, so no paid time is lost.

## Treasure Goblins
Now and then a treasure goblin runs through chat with a clinking sack. `!grab` it (one try each) and it shakes loose Madness and Stolen Coins for your satchel, sometimes a Portal Scrap. During a happy hour it's a gold rush: more goblins, double pay.

## The Remembrance
The Old Ones keep a quiet tally of who keeps the vigil. Chat on three different stream days in a month and you are remembered when the next one begins: Madness for everyone devoted, a rare gift and the title Herald of the Old Ones for the three most devoted, and gifts for a quarter of the rest. Support (subs, gifts, bits, raids, super chats) counts as devotion and adds chances, never a bigger gift. `!devotion` shows where you stand; `!devotion last` shows the last Remembrance.

## The Esoteric Order of the Tentacle
Above the cults stands the Order, and everyone who keeps coming back climbs it. Every stream day you chat on counts, and your own sub adds a day each month. Twenty-five ranks in five circles of five, the number of the Elder Sign: The Threshold, The Lantern Circle, The Inner Sanctum, The Drowned Choir and The Throne Beyond the Stars. Rank never drops, but stay away too long (fewer than three stream days in thirty) and you're astray: your perks wait until you return. At rank 13 you swear the Oath, and from then on the Order tithes you a Pact every week you're here; in the last circle, an Eldritch Pact each month too. `!order` shows where you stand; `!order ranks` shows the circles.

## Seasons & the Sunwait
The calendar changes the stream. On Moon Nights, around each full moon, the omens run wild, Beings stir sooner and Madness flows a little thicker. The Sunwait runs the six weeks before the winter solstice: the first stream of each week lights a candle, and its rune blesses everyone for two hours. `!season` and `!sunwait` show where we are. Some pages of the Ledger only open in these seasons.

## Pickman, Ghoul for Hire
Richard Upton Pickman, a ghoul in a tattered Hawaiian shirt, fetches forge materials for you. Send him on a job and he comes back by himself hours later, even when the stream is off, having eaten a little of the haul. Feed him and trade him spare curios and his **Favour** grows: he eats less, works faster and takes bigger orders. Now and then he paints you instead.

## Night Fishing
Fishing is a skill of its own. `!fish` casts a line (so does the *Go Fishing* channel point reward), the bite comes a little later, and the catch goes in your creel and your journal. Skill opens deeper waters, which also want better **bait**. Fish come Fine, Choice or Prime, and now and then one comes up **aberrant**. When the **Wreckage** drifts in, every cast hauls up something extra. Sell the catch to the Merchant from Leng or tip it into Tsathoggua. The Night Fishing page shows the waters and everything found so far.
